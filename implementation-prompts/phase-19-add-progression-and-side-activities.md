# Phase 19 — Add progression and side activities
Read rules and 16–18 handoffs. Give earnings a purpose and the world reasons to explore beyond mandatory deliveries.

## Result
Players unlock useful options without grinding through repetitive jobs or buying their way out of unfair defaults. Optional activities deepen residents and districts.

## Milestone A — Economy
Implement a single money ledger with typed credit/debit reasons, stable transaction IDs and no duplicate rewards. Distinguish shift earnings, spendable balance and story milestone rewards. Show an itemized shift recap with base pay, tips, bonuses, damage/repair and purchases. Keep fees proportional and allow a broke player to recover through normal play.

Set early prices so the first meaningful upgrade arrives after several successful deliveries rather than dozens. No mandatory campaign gate depends on an expensive purchase. Use bounded additive/multiplicative modifiers with an explicit application order and caps.

Implement at least five upgrades: insulated carrier, covered cargo rack, improved wet tires/brakes, slightly expanded van capacity and better route/forecast information. Keep baseline route guidance useful; an upgrade adds planning detail rather than repairing broken navigation. Show current/proposed effect and price before purchase. Upgrades require ownership/capacity validation and persistable IDs.

## Milestone B — Reputation and optional jobs
Track a small number of meaningful relationships/reputation categories, not a giant empty skill tree. Reliable service can unlock character conversation, favored routes and special orders. Bad deliveries cause recoverable reactions; do not permanently remove essential customers.

Implement at least five optional activity types using existing systems: a resident's lost-item errand, time trial with ghost/best-time data if feasible, photo/landmark discovery, community repair donation/errand, and special catering batch. Each needs an actual start, gameplay action, completion proof, reward and repeatability policy. For photo discovery, validate position/view/target rather than allowing a button anywhere to complete it. Keep time trials opt-in with no campaign penalty.

## Milestone C — Exploration and endgame
Place purposeful discoveries in all five districts: short character notes, recipes/collectible stamps or landmarks. Make map/journal progress readable without drowning the player in icons. Unlock endless shift jobs based on time/weather/district variety after campaign. Free roam has no food failure pressure unless the player opts into a job.

## Acceptance
Buy each upgrade and show the measured gameplay effect, including combined modifier caps. Cancel/repeat purchase cannot double-charge or duplicate ownership. Complete every side activity once and replay allowed ones without reward farming exploits. Finish campaign with no upgrades on Relaxed. Test zero-money repair/recovery and optional content after finale. Core order settlement remains exactly once.

Handoff: ledger schema, prices/modifiers, unlock IDs, optional quest catalog, repeatability and endgame policies for saves.
